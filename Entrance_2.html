The Creator

Sebastian Alonso Calderon was born on January 31, 1996 in the district capital of Lima, Peru. He grew up in a town called Downers Grove, Illinois. He played varsity soccer on his high school team his junior and senior year. And later attended the University of Illinois at Chicago for his bachelors of science in computer engineering. He graduated with honors (Cum Laude) and was president of an honor society. He was in 5 national honor societies and also graduated Top 10%. He is currently studying for his Masters of Science in Cybersecurity from the Georgia Institute of Technology.
